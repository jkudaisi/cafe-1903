Why 1903

Named for the espresso machine, not the building.

1903 marks the year Desiderio Pavoni of Milan perfected the espresso machine. To honor that history, the cafe pours Illy, a roaster founded in 1933 whose 1935 Illetta machine is widely considered the blueprint for modern espresso machines.

The original bones of the firehouse were kept, not gutted. Order in the compact front bar, then settle into the soaring all-season room with its big glass garage doors and communal table.
