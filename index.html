Why 1903

Named for the machine

1903 marks the year Desiderio Pavoni of Milan perfected the espresso machine. To honor that history, the cafe pours Illy, a roaster founded in 1933 whose 1935 Illetta machine is widely considered the blueprint for modern espresso machines. The original bones of the firehouse were kept, not gutted.
